## Word tables

The tables contain no copy of the source lists: they hold word prefixes,
suffixes, letter trigram statistics, word ranks and word subsets computed
from them, plus hand-made lists of frequent short words by the LayoutFixer
author.

### English — `*_en.txt`, `en-*.txt`, `ngrams-en.txt`, `short-words-en.txt`, `final-words-en.txt`

Derived from the 30,000 most frequent words of `count_1w.txt` compiled by
**Peter Norvig** ([norvig.com/ngrams](https://norvig.com/ngrams/)), which in
turn is derived from the **Google Web Trillion Word Corpus** described by
Thorsten Brants and Alex Franz and distributed by the
[Linguistic Data Consortium](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/LDC2006T13)
(LDC2006T13). Educational, personal and research use of this data is
permitted; commercial use may require a license from the LDC.

### Russian — `*_ru.txt`, `ru-*.txt`, `ngrams-ru.txt`, `short-words-ru.txt`

Derived from `50000-russian-words-cyrillic-only.txt` of
[hingston/russian](https://github.com/hingston/russian), a frequency-ordered
word list based on the **Leeds Internet Corpus** frequency list by
**Serge Sharoff**, Centre for Translation Studies, University of Leeds
([corpus.leeds.ac.uk](http://corpus.leeds.ac.uk/list.html)). The Russian
letter model is additionally trained on word forms generated by LayoutFixer's
own tools.

### Not included

Quality is measured with the [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords)
lists (OpenSubtitles 2018, CC BY-SA 4.0) by Hermit Dave. They are used for
evaluation only and are neither part of this repository nor of the program.
